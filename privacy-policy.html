What happens on Square is governed by Square's privacy policy, not ours.

01

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to aiso.dog (the "Site"), operated by AISOdog, a boutique AI search optimization consultancy ("we," "us," or "our"). It applies to visitors located anywhere, with particular attention to the rights afforded to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and its amendment, the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

05

Your Rights Under California Law

California residents have specific rights under CCPA and CPRA. We respect those rights. Because we collect minimal personal information, exercising most of them will return a short answer. That does not diminish the right.

Right to Know You may request a description of what personal information we hold about you, the categories of sources, and the purposes for which it is used.
Right to Delete You may request deletion of personal information we hold. We will comply unless retention is required by law or necessary to complete a transaction you requested.
Right to Correct You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing We do not sell or share personal information. There is nothing to opt out of. We will confirm this in writing upon request.
Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by CPRA.
Right to Non-Discrimination Exercising any privacy right will not affect your ability to use our services or the pricing you receive.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in the section below. We will respond within 45 days of a verified request. If we need more time (up to 90 days total), we will notify you of the extension and the reason.

You may designate an authorized agent to submit requests on your behalf. We may require written proof of authorization before processing an agent-submitted request.

06

How Long We Keep Information

Email correspondence is retained for as long as the business relationship is active and for a reasonable period thereafter (typically two years) in case of service disputes. Correspondence unrelated to a service engagement is deleted within 90 days of resolution.

Transaction records from Square are retained as required by California and federal tax law, generally seven years.

Server access logs maintained by our hosting provider are subject to that provider's retention schedule, which is typically 30 to 90 days. We have no control over this retention period.

07

Children Under 16

This site is intended for business professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

California Business and Professions Code Section 22575 requires disclosure of how we respond to Do Not Track signals. We do not track visitors across third-party websites, so Do Not Track signals have no additional effect on your experience here beyond what is already described in this policy.

The Challenge

Organic performance had followed the same pattern for years: a spike during peak season, then a steep drop-off. Rankings didn't compound. Content efforts were campaign-based and disconnected. The five-year keyword trajectory heading into the engagement was a net 3% gain — effectively flat, with significant volatility on either side of that number. The core question: how do you replace seasonal spikes with durable, always-on discoverability across both traditional search and an emerging AI search landscape?

Strategy

The approach replaced one-off content campaigns with a persistent optimization model built around three compounding pillars:

1. Topical Authority and Keyword Stabilization

Built seasonally complete topic ecosystems so the brand held rankings year-round — including during the months it had historically abandoned to competitors.

2. AI and SERP Optimization

Optimized content architecture for Google AI Overviews, answer engine extractability, and LLM discovery through entity clarity, structured data, and schema markup. Added off-site community presence to strengthen AI signaling.

3. Continuous Data-Driven Iteration

Used ranking signals, feature acquisition data, and AI sentiment tracking monthly to guide content prioritization — replacing gut-driven decisions with a measurable feedback loop.

Execution Highlights

  • Built topic clusters supporting year-round search intent across every season
  • Optimized priority pages for AI summarization and LLM extractability
  • Implemented schema markup and structured data across product and content pages
  • Transitioned from campaign SEO to persistent, always-on optimization
  • Established off-site community presence on forums to strengthen AI citation signals
  • Aligned product page content with Google Helpful Content standards
  • Tracked and reported AI citation share, sentiment, and SERP features monthly
  • Managed freelance copywriters across blog, technical, and product content workflows

AI Search Results

The AEO layer of this engagement produced the most significant documented results of the strategy. By the time reporting closed, the brand had established category-leading positions across every major AI search platform:

Outcome

Organic growth shifted from reactive and seasonal to compounding and durable. Keyword volatility was largely neutralized — the slow-season drop-offs that had defined five years of performance were eliminated. The brand entered 2025 with stronger topical authority, broader AI citation presence, and a content system built to continue growing without needing to restart from scratch each cycle.
